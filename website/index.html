What is School 21 CTF?

Hack & Learn

Practice reverse engineering, cryptography, web exploitation, forensics, and more in a safe environment.

Compete solo or form a squad of up to 4 players to tackle challenges together.

Win Prizes

Top teams and first-blood solvers earn swag, certificates, and School 21 leaderboard points.

All Levels Welcome

Challenges range from beginner-friendly to advanced, so everyone can contribute.
